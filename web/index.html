Cockpit Voice Modulation & Radiation
The pilot's voice is captured in the aircraft cockpit, conditioned, and modulated using Double Sideband Full Carrier AM (DSB-FC) onto VHF carriers between 118.000 and 136.975 MHz.
📡
Ground Intercept & Demodulation
Our custom receiver hardware intercepts the airband signal, suppresses out-of-band noise with LC filtering, amplifies through LNA, extracts audio via envelope detector, and digitizes to 16 kHz WAV.
🧠
Aviation Dataset & Emotion AI
Segmented transmissions are indexed into our real-time aviation corpus and classified across stress/calm/urgency states using CNN-BiLSTM and Vision Transformer architectures.
